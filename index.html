How this estimate works

    Before you rely on it

    This is a 2026 planning estimate. Actual payslips can differ because of personal deductions, family circumstances, benefits, contribution limits, local rules and payroll timing. The monthly equivalent is the annual figure divided by 12; Italian 13th and 14th payslips have different withholding, so the displayed per payslip amount is only an annual average.

    For German tax classes III, V and VI, household income or a second job can change the final tax assessment. Check a payroll specialist before accepting an offer. Class V/VI and private insurance are especially approximate.
