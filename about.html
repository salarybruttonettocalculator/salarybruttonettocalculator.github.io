How this site works

The calculator estimates take-home pay for employees in Germany and Italy. It also estimates the gross pay needed to reach a chosen net amount. The scientific calculator is a separate arithmetic tool.

Salary estimates

Your country, employment category, tax circumstances and salary inputs determine which of the available rules apply. The result breaks out estimated taxes and contributions, and shows annual and monthly amounts. The reverse calculation repeatedly estimates net pay from candidate gross salaries until it reaches the requested target.

Actual payslips can differ because of local taxes, deductions, insurance arrangements, employer payroll treatment, bonuses, family circumstances and changes during the year. Use the year and rate review date shown in the calculator. Check an official tax authority or qualified adviser for a binding answer.

The calculator runs in your browser. Salary entries are not submitted to our server by the calculator.

Bank account comparison

The bank list compares published standard account and card terms for residents in Germany. We selected providers with online signup and accessible card information. The descriptions indicate when a provider offers a payment account rather than a German bank account. Pricing, identity requirements and availability can change; follow the provider links to check the latest terms.
